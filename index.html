A simple and transparent process — admission is based on academic eligibility criteria specified for each programme. Candidates can apply without visiting the campus.

  1. Online Registration — Visit the LPU Online official portal and register using personal details such as name, email ID and mobile number. Login credentials are generated after successful registration.

Admission process 2026

LPU online admission 2026 is a clear and simple process. Everything is digital for the comfort of students. This allows learners to apply from any location. LPU Online ensures a fast and fair selection for everyone.

  1. Application Process 2026 — LPU Online admission 2026 begins with an online form. Students must register on the official portal first, providing a correct email and mobile number for all updates. The registration fee is only ₹600. Fill in your personal details and academic history carefully, and double-check all information before you submit the final form.
  2. Selection — LPU Online reviews all uploaded school and college documents. Document verification time is usually very quick. The university checks the academic eligibility of every candidate, and shortlisted students receive a confirmation on their registered email.
  3. Admission Confirmation — Complete the fee payment process to confirm your seat by paying the first semester fee. The university then provides your LPU Online login details, which you use to enter the study portal — containing all your books and video lessons — and get started.
