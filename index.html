Admission 2026

How to apply to LPU Online

A fully digital, merit-based process — no campus visit required.

ParticularDetail
Mode of applicationFully online
Admission criteriaMerit-based
Registration fee₹600
Registration deadline (Feb cycle)28 February 2026

Application steps

  1. Visit the admissions page and start your application.
  2. Register with your basic details.
  3. Log in to your admission dashboard.
  4. Fill the form — personal and academic information.
  5. Select your course and specialisation.
  6. Upload documents — certificates and ID.
  7. Pay the registration fee online.
  8. Submit and await confirmation.

Documents required

  • Class 10 & 12 marksheets and certificates
  • Graduation marksheet (for PG applicants)
  • Address proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Category certificate, if claiming a scholarship
